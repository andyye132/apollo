Apollo turns the sensors already in your phone into a quick daily health check-in, then helps you act on what it finds. No extra hardware.

What it does

Two kinds of service, one next step.

Tests you take when you want them, tracking that runs quietly if you opt in, and a clear path from a reading to what to do about it.

Four quick tests: heart rate, temperature, a lung check and calm sensing. A ring shows what's left today.

Opt-in passive tracking

All day, in the background

Steps and flights climbed, plus a prototype that counts blinks and gaze shifts. Nothing runs until you turn it on.

Tests you take

Four tests. About five minutes. Just your phone.

YOUR DAILY CHECK-IN

The ring fills one quarter per test. Tap once and Apollo runs the rest in order.

Opt-in passive tracking

Measured quietly. Nothing to start.

Turn it on once and Apollo keeps a gentle eye on your day.

  • Steps from the step counter, flights climbed from the barometer.
  • Eye tracking (prototype): blinks and gaze shifts per minute, early signs of fatigue or overload.
  • Counts only, never images. A notification shows whenever it's running.

Actionable insights

Care, picked for how you feel.

Apollo turns readings, chats and searches into a few short shelves, like a pharmacy aisle sorted for you.

  • "Based on your temperature": every shelf explains itself.
  • Real products with photos, sizes, prices and the FDA drug label.
  • Order through DoorDash or Uber Eats. You check out there.

When you need a person

The right place, open now.

Hospitals, urgent care, clinics and pharmacies near you, with hours, phone numbers and directions.

  • Filter by type, or show only what's open now.
  • "Which should I choose?" explains ER vs urgent care vs a clinic.
  • Emergency? Call 911 stays pinned to the screen.

Ask Apollo

A friendly guide who knows your numbers.

Do I have a fever?

Your last reading was a little warm. Want to recheck, or see what helps?

  • Explains readings against your own history and links to the right test.
  • Runs on the phone by default. Gemini mode is opt-in.
  • Emergencies are handled first, on the phone, with 911 and 988.
